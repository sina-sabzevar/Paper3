# CV — what you must fill in before sending

Build: `pdflatex sina_sabzevar_cv.tex` (run twice). Compiles on Overleaf as-is.
Red `[FILL: ...]` markers are placeholders. Set `\draftfalse` in the preamble to
preview without them — but **do not send the CV until every one is replaced.**

## Blocking (the CV is weaker than your competition without these)

**Numbers from your own work.** Not one number currently appears in your research
section. This is the single biggest gap:
- Schizophrenia study: dataset name, n patients / n controls, atlas, k discriminative
  regions, your metric vs. the correlation-FC baseline, imbalance ratio.
- Epilepsy study: n, scanner/protocol, subtype contrast, k regions, task metric.
- Generative augmentation: which metric improved, by how much, at what n.
- HPC: scale (subjects × parcellations, or GPU-hours).

**Neuroimaging toolboxes.** Your CV never names a single one. For a neuroimaging
applicant this reads as a red flag. Name what you actually used: fMRIPrep, FSL, SPM,
nilearn, CONN, Tetrad, SPM-DCM, AFNI, etc.

**Online presence** (website / Google Scholar / GitHub / ORCID). These are in the
header for a reason — advisors google you before replying. Currently they find nothing.

**Publication details.** ICSPIS DOI; journal name + submission month for the
manuscript under review; OHBM city and poster-vs-oral; the continual-learning
co-author list and target venue.

**TOEFL.** The old score (88, Speaking 20, Writing 20) is below the cutoff at many
programs and below TA-funding thresholds almost everywhere. Retake, then fill in.

## Changes made from the previous version, and why

- Header states one expertise instead of three fields.
- Publications grouped by type — the OHBM entry is now correctly labelled a
  conference abstract rather than "[Published]". Mislabelling it costs more
  credibility than the line is worth.
- Research bullets restructured to demand results, not activities.
- Industry role reframed around imbalanced learning + counterfactual estimation,
  so it supports the research claim instead of contradicting it.
- Skills cut from ~45 keywords to 4 grouped lines. Dropped YOLOv8, image
  segmentation, Simulink, R, C/C++, "Docker (Basic)" — each unrelated keyword
  subtracts from the one that matters.
- "Expected 2026" on the continual-learning paper is stale as of Sept 2026; set a
  realistic date or drop the entry.
